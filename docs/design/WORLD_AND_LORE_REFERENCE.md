# Connivances: World & Lore Reference

A living reference so flavor text across the simulator stays consistent with Bryan's canon.
Bryan owns this world; this doc just records what has been established so far and flags where the
game text needs to line up. Source material: the "First Betrayals" stories (Falla's fall, the
childhood of Falla and Laere). Update as more canon is written.

## Tone & vibe
Japanese-key high fantasy (matches the art direction): grounded, sensory, character-driven, with a
class-structured society and orders of magic. Emotional and personal at the small scale (a friendship,
a merchant's shop, a father collapsing), with a slow-burning conspiracy underneath. The throughline is
BETRAYAL: trusted structures and people turn on you. The game's twist mechanics (a tutorial that
betrays you, "the tool that saves becomes the weapon") are the mechanical echo of this theme.

## Places
- **Theipeann** — a **village**, the home of Falla and Laere. Offset from the kingdom. NOT a city or
  the capital. (Fix any game text that calls it a city.) It has a mercantile quarter and an Artist
  district. Used in-game as the settlement the Warrior band patrols (Sundering Stone) and the home the
  Four Horsemen ride on (Long Winter).
- **The kingdom** — the "ancient eastern kingdom," so far UNNAMED. Theipeann sits apart from it.
- **Echec mountain** — where Falla falls; crags and a piney forest below.
- **Ancil** — a village whose cliff fronts collapsed ("The Great Portent," a famous scrying event).

## Characters
- **Falla** (also spelled **Fallah** in places; the `falla` card art). An **Augury** initiate,
  daughter of the merchant Jaord. Her gift: she reads and CHANGES the **Auspice** to bend a moment to
  advantage. In-world this IS the game's Auspice mechanic, personified. She sees portents/visions; a
  hidden society appears in candlelit visions. Recruited into the Augury order by **Rose**.
- **Laere** (also spelled **Leare**). Falla's best friend, of the **Conjurer** order. Third-born of a
  family of scribes; an autodidact savant of the kingdom's knowledge. Summoned a great bird from
  forbidden old texts, something few Conjurers ever manage. Embodies the Conjurer order (summoning).
- **Austis** — the **Minikowa** (great summoned bird) Laere raised. Bonded to Falla.
- **Jaord** — Falla's father; runs the shop "Better than Goods" in the mercantile quarter.
- **Kelvan** — Laere's father, a scribe; collapses from a **mannice** bite (a rare neurotoxic
  creature; antivenom is turmeric + trigonostemon root). Laere's deduction of the bite is his first
  spark of brilliance.
- **The Grace Master of Divination** — head trainer of the Augury order; wants to make Falla the
  greatest Augur ever, unaware of the conspiracy.
- **The head of the Augury order** — secretly part of the hidden plot; sabotages Falla (plants a
  false friend, drugs her visions, sabotages her Minikowa ride) because her raw foresight threatens
  the plan.
- **The King** (the `kn` "King's Note" card is his). A would-be benevolent ruler who spent DECADES
  ending not foreign wars but his own people's internal wars: Lords warring over land rights,
  provisions, and water channels, amid raids, treason, plague, starvation, and genocide. He brokered
  an imperfect peace (border disputes and old atrocities still smolder, but a common bond to heal
  binds the houses). His tragedy: the children who survived those decades grew hard, strong-willed,
  hungry to build and become something, and now push back against his policies. He wanted to make
  rival houses see each other's plight; kingship does not reward that ideal. So he reached for a
  common enemy to unite them, and opened the Portal (below). A sympathetic architect of catastrophe.

## The orders (map to game factions)
- **Augury** (Augury Mages) — scrying, birds, portents, divination; masters of the **Auspice**.
- **Conjurers** — summon creatures from old texts and ritual (the Minikowa; the Brood).
- **Builders** — the third order named; craft and stonework (a torch-lit corridor "clearly crafted by
  the builders" appears in Falla's vision). Siege/permanents in-game.
- **Warriors** — the soldiery (in-game the swarm/Muster). Not yet foregrounded in the stories.

## The Connivance (the title, and the throughline)
A hidden, UNNAMED order plots to "solidify the class structures" of the kingdom. They meet in candlelit
stone rooms; a "grand conjurer" leads a rite ("Tonight we celebrate and inspire our works into the
future"). Falla's innate foresight could expose them, so they betray and break her. Her fall, her
vision, and her ability are "the impetus of connivance." The game's escalating threat and the twist
finales are the surface of this buried plot. **The King works WITH this secret society** (their aims
align: he wants a unifying common enemy, they want to cement the class order).

## The Portal's true origin (the central secret)
**The King and the secret society opened the Portal themselves.** Needing a common enemy to bind his
fractious people, the King used old scripts from the CONJURER orders (he was not even sure the magic
was real), and together with the society obtained the **summoning stones** and built the Portal. When
it struck to life they could not believe it: REAL magic had only just begun to be known and controlled.
So the Seeping Portal / Creeping Doom is not a natural calamity, it is a false-flag catastrophe that
got out of hand. (Ties to the Sundering Stone ward-stone, whose seal is "woven from the same veil-magic
that has recently begun to spread." Summoning stones and ward-stones are the same order of deep magic.)

## Why possessions exist (canonical justification of the Conjurer tutorial)
The people had **lost their ties to deep magic**; it is only now, newly, being rediscovered and barely
controlled. So anyone conjuring is playing with forces they do not understand, and it has repercussions:
wild-magic backlash, i.e. **possessions**. This is the in-world reason the Conjurer ("Summoner's
Plight") tutorial punishes dabbling. Keep Conjurer flavor in this key: magic is young, hungry, and
imperfectly leashed; every summon is a hand into the dark.

## Deep cuts that already exist as game content
- **"Food for the masses"** — an accidental conjuration of a **brood of hens** that saved the Artist
  district from starvation. This is the **Brood** conjuration's origin. Use this when flavoring Broods.
- **"The portals"** — Falla's era is "by the time of the portals." The Seeping Portal / Creeping Doom
  was OPENED DELIBERATELY by the King and the secret society (summoning stones) as a false-flag common
  enemy; see "The Portal's true origin" above. The veil-magic spreading through the land (Sundering
  Stone ward-stone lore) is the same young, barely-controlled deep magic.
- **The Auspice** — Falla's personal gift; treat auspice-shifting as a rare, prized talent in flavor.

## Spelling & naming conventions (CANONICAL, decided 2026-09-24)
- **Fallah** (with the H) is the canonical spelling of her name. The card art FILE stays `falla` (do
  not rename the asset), but all display text and prose use **Fallah**.
- **Laere** (A before the E: L-a-e-r-e) is canonical. Never "Leare".
- **Theipeann** is the settled spelling for the village.
