# 😈 The Evil Ideas Bank

A running bank of the devious, twisty, gotcha-shaped game ideas we've had for Connivances,
kept so we can review them for viability when we want **fresh content or a new twist**. Not a
backlog of features to build; a menu of *nasty surprises* to pull from.

**How to use this doc:** when a genuinely evil/twisty idea comes up in any conversation, add
an entry here (even a half-formed one). When Bryan wants to plan fresh content, we review the
bank together and pick what's viable. Entries link to a fuller spec when one exists.

**What counts as "evil":** hidden mechanics, lull-then-betray reveals, "you don't know what you
actually want" gotchas, systems that turn on the player, rug-pulls that are *earned* rather than
cheap, secret win/lose inversions. The good kind of mean.

## Entries

### 1. The Sundering Stone's hidden second phase
**Hook:** "You want the Minotaur to win, but you don't know you want the Minotaur to win."
The ward-stones hold magic only the Minotaur can break; players think they must protect the
seal (lose if it shatters), but secretly *letting* him break it starts a hidden phase-2 boss
with **alternative, richer rewards**. Loss-that's-secretly-a-second-chance.
**Status:** Idea, parked. **Spec:** `docs/design/SUNDERING_STONE_HIDDEN_PHASE.md`.
**Viability:** High. Small build on the existing seal-stone tutorial (reuse the finale
phase-flag pattern). Main risk: hinting without revealing, and keeping the safe path winnable.

### 2. The Giving Tree "Feed me" finale (Little Shop of Horrors)
**Hook:** You nurse a dying seedling to bloom over six wholesome stages; then it turns
carnivorous and demands to be fed. The auspice control you used to *save* it is now the only way
to *kill* it. Every season that raised it now feeds the monster. "I taught it to want this."
**Status:** BUILT (behind `TREE_FINALE`, default on). **Spec:** `docs/design/GIVING_TREE_FINALE_SPEC.md`.
**Viability:** Proven; live and verified. The template for "the tool that saves becomes the weapon."

### 3. The Long Winter "Risen" resurrection finale
**Hook:** You grind down the Four Horsemen and finally kill Death, thinking you've won; instead
all four rise as one wraith-host and ride together, wounded only by ANY attack, on a hard clock.
The kill that should end it *starts* the real fight.
**Status:** BUILT. **Spec:** `docs/design/LONG_WINTER_FINALE_SPEC.md`.
**Viability:** Proven. A "your victory was the trigger" escalation; good pattern to reuse.

### 4. The Conjurer's wild-magic backlash ("Clear the Haunted House")
**Hook:** Summoning feels great two or three times, then the veil doesn't close and your own
power infests the marketplace. The thing you were enjoying is the thing now hurting you, and
also your only way out.
**Status:** BUILT (Conjurer Training: The Summoner's Plight).
**Viability:** Proven. Core is a lull-then-backlash; the deeper "Feed me"-style finale for it is
still on ice (`docs/design/GIVING_TREE_FINALE_SPEC.md` is the model).
