# Connivances. Faction Identities (designer-authored, 2026-07-22)

The **spirit** of each faction, in the designer's own words. This is authoritative
design intent for card work; check any card proposal against the faction's fantasy
here before proposing mechanics. Not yet folded into the Master Instructions MD.

---

## Builders
> "May be the most straight forward faction, they build permanents into play that add
> passive boosts and also have direct attack abilities per turn. They are meant to be
> **low, slow and steady**, but their **engines of war can be wielded easily by other
> factions**. They also repair."

- Permanents-first; passive boosts + per-turn direct attack.
- Deliberately slow; the payoff is durability and compounding, not tempo.
- **Their engines are meant to be shared.** Siege buildings are the game's only
  recurring-ATK permanents, and other factions are expected to use them.
- Repair is theirs (17 repair uses across the guild).

## Augury Mages
> "Built for **Auspice manipulations**. The augury mages control the global passive
> abilities of the auspice and eventually can wield it to great effect to thin decks,
> starve out other players in PVP, reconstitute food supplies with harvest, give card
> draw. They are mostly a **support class**… but with Necromancer and enough augury
> mages, you can **quick burst a lot of damage**. This is a **fast ramp**, but without
> that one window, they are **light on attack**. They do **not create permanents** and
> their **hands are recycled quickly** because of their abilities to draw other augury
> mages from the draw deck."

- Global-state controllers (Auspice), deck-thinning, draw engine, food via Harvest.
- PvP: starve opponents by denying Harvest / forcing Famine.
- One burst window (Necromancer + mass Augury Mages); otherwise low ATK.
- Constraints: **no permanents**, fast self-recycling hands.
- 🔧 **Open designer request:** add *another* mid-game attack method for them.

## Farmers
> "Clearly the food experts, they are all around **food support for every faction**, but
> the **warriors can use them to start a sort of pitchfork army or angry mob**, the
> likes of which can overwhelm."

- Universal support: food for everyone.
- **Signature crossover:** Farmers + Warriors = the pitchfork army. Already live via
  **Trainer (w3)**; "Farmers count as Warriors this turn."
- Implies the mob is a *sanctioned* archetype, not an accident.

## Warriors
> "Also a faction that does **not build permanents**, with the **Dojo being the
> exception**. The Dojo is where the warriors **train for the fight and convert their
> influence into attack**. However, it feels like a **very narrow ramp**, but I also
> **can not allow them to draw into 4 or 5 attack in the early game** if the ramp breaks."

- No permanents except Dojo.
- **Dojo's stated purpose = the Influence→ATK pipe.** This settles that a
  Warrior-gated conversion is thematically correct; the earlier rejection of
  "Warriors print ATK" was about **magnitude**, not the concept.
- Identity is **MASS, not individual power**. Negotiator/Warlord scale with warrior
  *count*; Trainer converts Farmers into bodies. The fantasy is *many overwhelming*.
- ⚠️ **HARD BALANCE CONSTRAINT:** must not reach **4–5 ATK in the early game**. Any
  reprice or new pipe must be provably incapable of an early spike.
- Designer's own read: the ramp is **too narrow**.

## Conjurers
> "The summoners of the world playing with **wild magics** to summon permanents into
> play. This is also why the **curses and possessions are hidden throughout the deck**
> representing the **untamed magics**."

- Permanents via summoning; the creature/ATK tier.
- **Possessions are the faction's built-in risk**, not an external attack; the price
  of playing with power you don't control. Consistent with the separate ruling that
  Possessions are wild-magic backlash, not portal monsters.

## Cross-faction notes
- **Only Builders and Conjurers make permanents.** Warriors (bar Dojo) and Augury
  Mages do not; so in Creeping Doom, where monsters target permanents, those two
  guilds present almost no board for monsters to attack.
- **Sharing is intended:** Builder engines are explicitly wieldable by others, which
  supports letting non-Builder guilds reach Siege.
- Warriors and Augury Mages are the two guilds the designer currently considers
  under-served on attack; see `cards/WARRIOR_GUILD_REVIEW.md`.
