Connivances Card Frames

Your card frame language, rebuilt in code from the 2011 & 2016 printouts and the physical deck; one faithful template per card class, ready to carry Midjourney art.

Every frame here is decoded from your own cards, not invented: border colour encodes card class, the ribbon & totem encode guild, and the corner marks carry VP, cost, combat & defence. The art windows are deliberately empty; that slot is exactly what the art pipeline fills.
